0% to the Platinum

⚠ One missable trophy. Positive Mental Attitude (meditate at all 18 spots) is the only trophy two independent sources flag as possibly missable — the Lucia balcony meditation in Chapter 1 is reported to disappear after that chapter. Keep a manual save at the start of every chapter: the game is reported to have no mission replay. All other trophies are earned by playing on.

Road to the Platinum — five stages

  1. Stage 1 — Play the story. Chapters 1–4 and the finale earn 13 story trophies, including the gold for the final mission. Save at the start of each chapter. Do the Chapter 1 balcony meditation before Chapter 1 ends.
  2. Stage 2 — Stranger strands. Finish the final mission for Andy, Kenton, Billy, Randy, Tyrone and Bret, plus two more stranger strands, for the silver Strangers & Freaks.
  3. Stage 3 — Activities & skill. Gold all 42 Shooting Range challenges, win 20 races and drone races at all 14 Top Fun locations, pull off 250 stunts, beat Cal at fishing, and finish 50 challenges.
  4. Stage 4 — Collectibles & exploration. 3 card sets, WAiNK at 100 locations, all 18 meditation spots, 100 vehicles, all 52 areas of Leonida, all 8 garages, 10 vehicles in the Scooter Bros app.
  5. Stage 5 — Cleanup & 100%. Robberies (5 bank vaults, 9 gangs, armored trucks), $250,000 laundered, $100,000 spent, 500 Criminal Profile actions — then the gold A Long Road (100% completion) and the Platinum, Vice Got a Price!
